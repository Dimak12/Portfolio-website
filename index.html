01About

I am an MSc Artificial Intelligence candidate at the University of Cape Town, coming from a computer engineering background at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology.

My dissertation looks at explaining the predictions of neural models using logic-based reasoning that can handle defaults and exceptions. The aim is explanations that a domain expert can actually read and challenge, rather than another opaque score.

Before the MSc I completed a Diploma, a BEng Tech (Cum Laude) and a BEng Tech Honours in Computer Engineering, where my thesis applied deep learning to intrusion detection in hybrid software-defined networks. In industry I worked with Koolboks Nigeria on backend and intelligent systems development.

I am interested in AI that holds up outside the lab: models that are explainable, robust to messy data and built with the people who will use them. I have also volunteered at IEEE GLOBECOM 2024 and AfricArena 2023.

Research interests

  • Explainable and neuro-symbolic AI
  • Knowledge representation and nonmonotonic reasoning
  • Spatio-temporal graph neural networks
  • NLP for low-resource African languages
  • AI for network security
